Before you email

A few quick things you can check yourself, which solve most issues:

  • Update the App. Most bugs are fixed in the latest version — open the App Store or Google Play and check for updates.
  • Restart the App. Fully close it and reopen. Clears most transient glitches.
  • Check your connection. The App needs internet for sign-in, pod sync, and stats. Guest mode and an in-progress match continue to work offline.
  • Sign out and back in. Resolves auth-related issues after account changes.

Reporting a bug

To help us fix things fast, please include:

  • Your device (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro, Pixel 8)
  • Operating system version (e.g. iOS 18.2, Android 14)
  • App version (Settings → About)
  • A short description of what you did, what you expected, and what actually happened
  • A screenshot or screen recording if possible

Frequently asked

How do I invite a friend to my pod?

Go to Home → My Pods, open a pod you own, and tap the invite button. You'll get a short code and a share sheet — send it to your friend, and they can redeem it from My Pods → Join.

Can I use the app without signing up?

Yes. Tap Continue as Guest on the welcome screen. Guest data is stored only on your device, so it won't sync across phones and is lost if you uninstall. Sign up any time to keep your history safe.

Why can't I find a specific commander?

Card data comes from Scryfall. If a card is brand new, it may take a day or two to be indexed. Spellings must match the English card name — partner and background names should be entered separately.

I think a win rate or stat looks wrong.

Stats are computed from the matches you've logged. Check History for any unfinished or duplicate matches. Email us if you've confirmed the data is clean and the number still looks off.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app and go to Settings → Delete account. Confirm the prompt and your account, sign-in credentials, decks, players, and personal match history will be permanently deleted. Pod history shared with other players is anonymized rather than removed. If you can't access the app, email support@aboutagency.mx from the email address on your account and we'll process the request within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.
